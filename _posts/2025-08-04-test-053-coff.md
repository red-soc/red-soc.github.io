## Background

Every field is little-endian on x86, which matters when you read raw bytes from a file. Writing the parser as a set of small functions made each structure easy to verify separately.

```c
Elf64_Ehdr *eh = (Elf64_Ehdr *)map;
printf("entry: 0x%lx\n", eh->e_entry);
```
