<p>Red-Soc Blog is a personal research notebook covering <strong>security research</strong>, <strong>OS Hacking</strong>, and the technical details in between. Most posts are deep dives into Windows internals, hypervisor design, binary formats, and the boundaries that modern anti-cheat systems watch.</p>

<p>The goal is simple: write down what I learn while it is still fresh, with enough detail that future-me (and anyone else reading) can reproduce the result. Expect code snippets, packet dumps, the occasional failed experiment, and notes on the tooling that makes the work easier.</p>

<p>When I am not reversing binaries or writing about OS, I am usually tinkering with side projects, reading research papers, or filing notes under one of the <a href="{{ '/categories/' | relative_url }}">categories</a> on this site.</p>

<p>The best way to reach me is through GitHub: <a href="https://github.com/Red-Soc">github.com/Red-Soc</a>.</p>
